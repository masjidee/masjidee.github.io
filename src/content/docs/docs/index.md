Masjidee brings your mosque’s prayer times, activities and learning together. These guides cover the current Android pilot and the connected learning website.

**New here?** Start with [Getting started](./guides/getting-started/). If you need an installation file, [read about pilot access](../get-app/).

## Find your next step

- [Choose your mosque](./guides/choose-mosque/) and see the right local schedule.
- [Set prayer reminders](./guides/prayers/) for the congregation times you follow.
- [Follow community calendars](./guides/calendars/) for activities that matter to you.
- [Understand check-ins](./guides/check-ins/) before enabling automatic detection.
- [Open learning and Friday sermons](./guides/learning/) from the app.
- [Get help](./guides/troubleshooting/) when something does not look right.

## A note about the pilot

Android features are still being tested on real phones. iPhone support is planned, not released. Menu names can change between pilot versions; use the latest build your organizer has given you.

Prayer schedules come from your mosque. Location events and calendar matches are useful clues, not proof that someone attended a prayer or activity.
