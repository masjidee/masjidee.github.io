title: Mosque settings & calendars
description: A practical guide for people granted mosque configuration access.
---
Mosque settings are for authorized people. Being a teacher or content creator does not automatically provide this access.

## Which administrator role?

- **Mosque Administrator:** settings for a specifically assigned mosque.
- **Global / Jamat Mosque Administrator:** mosque settings across the configured mosque scope, rather than one assigned mosque.
- **Application Administrator:** manages user access and assigns the above permissions in the web application.

Mosque configuration access does not automatically allow viewing other people’s attendance reports.

## Review location settings

1. In Masjidee, open **☰ → Mosque settings**.
2. Select the mosque if your permission covers more than one.
3. Review its name, timezone, address, coordinates and geofence radius.
4. If appropriate, use **Detect current position** while physically at the mosque and grant foreground location access.
5. Check the position carefully before saving.

A phone fix can be imprecise. Avoid moving a geofence to your current location when you are not at the mosque. People may need to refresh detection settings after a site change.

## Add a Google Calendar

Add the calendar using its public Google Calendar identifier or supported URL. Choose the mosque’s timezone and relevant categories, such as Salat or Jamat. Calendars must be accessible to the importer; sharing a private calendar’s viewing URL alone does not make it public.

For prayer calendars, specify whether the times are prayer-begins or congregation times. Review the discovered event titles in the import grid and map them to the appropriate prayer names. Combined **Zohar and Asr** or **Maghrib and Isha** mappings are supported; special prayers depend on the available catalog.

Preview the import, inspect dates and times, then activate the reviewed calendar. Re-import after changes and review import errors rather than assuming a button press succeeded. Future phone refreshes can reflect the updated schedule, but an offline phone cannot instantly receive it.

## Tell your community

Ask users to select the correct mosque and follow the calendar categories they want. Publish calendar corrections through the source and re-import them; a learning-content edit does not change congregation times.
