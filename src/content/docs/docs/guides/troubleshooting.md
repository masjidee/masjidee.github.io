## The app is slow to connect

During the pilot, the server can sleep when unused. Opening the app or sending a background request can wake it. You may briefly see a loading state, a saved schedule or a connection error if startup takes too long.

Wait a little, then use **Retry connection** or refresh. Do not assume a connection error means your account was deleted. Previously scheduled notifications and locally queued check-ins have different behavior from live server screens.

## Prayer times or activities are missing

Check the mosque name, refresh the schedule, read any freshness message and review your followed calendar categories. Ask the mosque administrator whether the relevant calendar was published, reviewed and activated. The app cannot fill in unpublished times.

## Automatic arrival or departure was missed

Check **☰ → Automatic detection / device status**. Confirm background-location permission and the selected mosque. Review phone battery restrictions and whether the app was force-stopped. Reopen it, and use manual In/Out if needed.

Android can delay geofence callbacks. Device-status information does not guarantee every crossing will be detected. Share exact times privately with your organizer rather than posting location history publicly.

## A notification did not appear

Check Android’s notification permission, Masjidee’s relevant setting, silent/Do Not Disturb controls and the schedule’s freshness. Arrival/departure alerts and prayer departure reminders are separate settings.

## A check-in is visible but attendance is not

Raw event upload and attendance interpretation are separate steps. Reporting needs its server job to be enabled and run successfully. Missing or ambiguous evidence may remain incomplete. Ask your organizer if processing appears stuck.

## Sermon Listen or Watch is disabled

The imported source did not supply a usable link for that language and format. Use the **official Alislam page** action. If a link opens but does not play, tell your organizer the sermon date, language and whether audio or video failed.

## Question audio differs between phone and web

Confirm the same account, concept and individual question, then check your pilot app version. Not every question has audio. Send your organizer the exact question title and the symptom.

## Android blocks installation

Send the organizer the exact security message and phone/Android version. Do not turn off broad account or device protection just to bypass a warning.

## Still need help?

[Contact your pilot organizer or send safe website feedback](../../../support/). Include the app version and error text, keeping account and location information private.
