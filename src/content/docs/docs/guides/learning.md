## Open learning

Tap **Learn**. Masjidee opens the student learning interface with your existing app account; you should not need a second sign-in for that handoff.

Browse the tag folders, open a concept and use its flashcards or questions. Open assignments to find work available to you and resume your progress. Question audio controls appear when a recording or audio link has been attached to that question.

Masjidee’s embedded learning view is **student-only**, even if your account has administrator or teacher permissions on the separate web application.

If a lesson or audio is missing, compare the question and account you are using. Audio belongs to individual questions, so it is not guaranteed on every question or concept overview.

## Friday sermons

Home places the latest two discovered published sermons beneath the next-prayer area. Each card can offer **Urdu audio, English audio, Urdu video** and **English video** when the official media links are available.

Tap **Listen** or **Watch** to open the appropriate player. A greyed-out action means a usable link was not available; the app does not guess a media URL. The official Alislam page is the fallback for viewing the source or other languages.

Playback is started by you. Background playback and downloaded/offline sermon media are not promised in the pilot. External providers may open their own browser experience if an embedded player cannot be used.

Sermons are linked from official Alislam sources. Masjidee is an independent community project, not the publisher of those sermons.
