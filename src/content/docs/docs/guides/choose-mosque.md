title: Choose your mosque
description: Find your home mosque or temporarily select a visiting mosque.
---
Your selected mosque appears at the top of Masjidee. Prayer times, activities and location settings follow this selection.

## Find or change a mosque

1. Open **☰ → Change mosque** or **Mosques near me**.
2. Choose **My organization** or **All mosques**.
3. Search, or tap **Use my location** to find nearby options and allow location access if you want proximity search.
4. Review the name and address. The map action can help confirm the right place.
5. Tap **Use this mosque**.

Only mosques configured in this system are included. “All mosques” is not a complete worldwide directory. Distance is an estimate; do not treat it as live traffic or a guaranteed journey time.

## Visiting another mosque

Choosing a visiting mosque changes what the app shows. It does not automatically rewrite your organization memberships. Manage those separately in your profile or through your application administrator.

After changing mosques, review prayer reminders and automatic detection. Refresh the new mosque’s settings rather than assuming the old configuration is still appropriate.

## No mosque or schedule appears

A mosque may need to be classified, linked to a location or given an active calendar by an administrator. Contact your organizer if it cannot be found.
