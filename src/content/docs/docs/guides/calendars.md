Mosques can publish separate Google Calendars for prayers and community activities. Masjidee groups these using categories such as **Salat, Jamat, Amila, Khuddam, Atfal, Nasirat** and **Lajna**.

## Follow categories

1. Open the **person icon**.
2. Find **Follow calendar categories**.
3. Choose the categories you want for the selected mosque.
4. Tap **Save followed categories**.

These are Masjidee preferences. They do not subscribe your Google account or add calendars to your phone’s separate calendar app.

## Find an activity

Home shows upcoming activities. Open **Events** to browse the available day, week or month views and date controls. Check the selected mosque, event date and displayed timezone.

If an event is missing, check your followed categories first. A published calendar also needs to be active and successfully imported by the mosque administrator.

## Calendar updates

Schedules refresh from published sources. Updates are not instant on every phone: an offline or sleeping device may display older saved information. Use the refresh action and check freshness messages when plans change.
