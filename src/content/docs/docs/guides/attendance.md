## Open your report

In the **Masjidee phone app**, open **☰ → Attendance reports → My attendance**. This is separate from the raw records under **My check-ins / privacy**.

Reports show interpreted visits, entry/exit information, incomplete or uncertain evidence and possible prayer matches. A server job processes observations periodically when enabled; it may run late. A successful check-in does not mean a visit report appears immediately.

## What a match means

A prayer match compares visit observations with the mosque’s published congregation schedule. It is a schedule-based inference or a self-report, not proof of attendance. Missing entries/exits, overlapping devices and ambiguous schedules can prevent a clear match.

## Sharing

Sharing with authorized mosque reporters is **off by default**. Use the attendance privacy controls if you want to share. Authorized mosque reports include sharing users only; they are not a complete list of members or proof that someone was absent.

Application administrators have separate privileged troubleshooting access to retained records. Mosque settings permissions alone do not grant that reporting access.

## Retention and deletion

Raw entry/exit observations expire after **30 days**. Derived attendance summaries remain **until deleted**. Use the delete-history control to remove live raw and derived attendance history. Historical backup copies follow the service’s backup retention process.

The web application also has an [administrator-only diagnostic report](../../admin/attendance/). It is not a student report or part of the embedded Learn screen.
