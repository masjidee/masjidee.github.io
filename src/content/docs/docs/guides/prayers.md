title: Prayer times & reminders
description: See published congregation times and choose fixed-lead departure reminders.

## See the schedule

Home highlights the **next congregation**. Open **All prayer times** or the **Prayers** tab for more times. Check the mosque name and schedule freshness message.

Masjidee uses the Google Calendar information your mosque publishes and its administrator reviews. It does not invent missing prayer times. When the mosque combines Zohar/Asr or Maghrib/Isha, both can display the same congregation time.

Special prayers such as Jumu’ah, Eid and Taraweeh depend on the published calendar and its mappings. Contact your mosque administrator about a wrong or missing time.

## Turn on a departure reminder

1. Open the **person icon → Prayer departure reminders**.
2. Choose how many minutes before congregation to be reminded.
3. Select the prayers you want.
4. Enable reminders and allow notifications when prompted.
5. Review the status and next scheduled reminder.

The lead time is configurable from **0 to 120 whole minutes**. It is a fixed number of minutes, **not** a live traffic, distance, walking or driving calculation. Reminders start off until you enable them.

Only eligible, reviewed congregation times create reminders. Missing, stale, incomplete or conflicting schedules may prevent scheduling. Refresh the mosque schedule when asked.

## Offline and phone behavior

Already scheduled reminders can appear without an internet connection. Future reminders need fresh schedule information. Android’s background and notification controls can delay delivery, especially after force-stop or battery restrictions.

An offline phone cannot know that a calendar time was just changed. Reopen Masjidee and refresh before relying on a recently edited schedule. Always confirm important times with your mosque.
