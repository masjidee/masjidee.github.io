---
title: Find the Qibla
description: Use the foreground Qibla tool and understand compass limitations.
---
Open **☰ → Qibla direction**, or the Qibla action where shown on Home or Prayers.

Allow foreground location access if requested so the app can calculate direction from your position. Follow any compass or calibration guidance shown on your phone.

Keep the phone away from magnets, metal surfaces and electronic interference. Compass readings can drift indoors. Use the result as a guide and confirm the mosque’s established prayer direction if you are unsure.

Opening the Qibla finder is not the same as enabling background mosque check-ins. [Read about separate permissions](../privacy/).
